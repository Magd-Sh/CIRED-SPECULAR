Interactive maps

Companion material to Mapping employment change in the European automotive industry, 2006–2025 — Majd Shamashan, CIRED, PEPR SPLEEN (SPECULAR project).

Each map opens in the browser. Hover a region or a plant to read its name and its employment figure; use the layer control in the top right to switch layers on and off.

Where the employment is

  • Automotive employment by region, 2023 Regions outside France at NUTS-2 level from Eurostat; France at département level from URSSAF, on its own colour scale. National borders are drawn heavier than regional ones. Tracked plants appear as circles sized by employment.
  • Change in regional employment, 2012–2023 Where the industry grew and where it contracted over the period.
  • Regional employment, 2019 Mid-period reference year.

Plants and the value chain

  • The automotive value chain in Europe Tracked plants, car assembly sites recorded by the IEA, and the supplier, component, battery and steel sites behind them. Assembly sites with no tracked plant nearby are marked in red.
  • Tracked plants over regional employment How much of each region's automotive employment sits inside the plants followed in this study.
  • All tracked plants The full plant panel with group, location and employment.

Electrification

  • Battery manufacturing in France Commune-level battery employment from URSSAF. The activity class covers all batteries, so it cannot identify electric-vehicle cells on its own; the known cell plants are marked separately.

Data coverage

  • Eurostat regional coverage, 2019 Which regions report a figure, which are withheld as confidential, and which are missing.
Employment data: Eurostat structural business statistics, URSSAF (France), and a plant-level panel built for this study. Vehicle data: European Environment Agency CO2 monitoring database (Regulation (EU) 2019/631). Facility locations: International Energy Agency.
